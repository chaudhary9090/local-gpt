ENTERPRISE INFORMATION SECURITY & LOCAL AI POLICY

Document Reference: SEC-POL-2026-v4
Effective Date: October 2026
Owner: Inno, Enterprise Administration Team

1. Objective & Scope
This policy defines the acceptable use, data confidentiality standards, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) governance for on-premises small language models (SLMs) and corporate document systems.

2. On-Premises & Local Data Privacy Standards
2.1 All confidential corporate documents, customer contracts, and employee records must be processed strictly within on-premises or private local instances.
2.2 Vector embeddings must be stored in encrypted local vector databases (ChromaDB / SQLite Vector) without transmitting raw chunk embeddings to unapproved external endpoints.
2.3 The system utilizes local inference engines (Ollama SLMs) guaranteeing 100% data residency and zero internet telemetry for proprietary documents.

3. Document Chunking & Ingestion Standards
3.1 Text splitting parameter defaults must be configured with a chunk size of 1000 characters and an overlap of 150 characters to prevent loss of context across semantic boundaries.
3.2 Documents accepted include PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, and CSV formats.
3.3 Parsing must preserve section hierarchies, clause numbers, and metadata tags (source filename, page number, timestamp).

4. Anti-Hallucination & Retrieval Guardrails
4.1 The RAG prompt must enforce strict adherence to retrieved context:
    "Answer ONLY using the provided context. If the answer is not found in the context, say: 'I couldn't find that information in the document.'"
4.2 When context confidence is low or when no matching chunks exceed the similarity threshold, the model must explicitly notify the user rather than inferring facts.

5. Approval, Disapproval, and Human Review Escalation
5.1 Agreements analyzed by the SLM are scored across three categories:
    - APP (Approved): Standard clauses conforming to standard liability caps, mutual indemnities, and balanced termination rights.
    - DIS (Disapproved): High-risk clauses containing uncapped liabilities, unilateral broad indemnities, or punitive non-cancellation terms.
    - REVIEW (Human Review): Ambiguous terms, modified cure periods, or non-standard governing laws requiring attorney escalation.
